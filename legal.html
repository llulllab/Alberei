Privacy Policy

Article 1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Alberei, a product of Llull Lab (Paris, France), handles personal data. Llull Lab is the data controller. You can reach us at contact@llulllab.com. Alberei is designed to operate with minimal data collection.

Article 2 Sign-in and Account Data

Alberei signs you in with a link sent to your email address. There is no Google Sign-In and no third-party identity provider. When you request a sign-in link, we store:

  • Your email address
  • An internal user identifier
  • A language preference
  • Whether your email address has been verified
  • The status of your account, and the dates your record was created and last updated
  • The sign-in link itself: a single-use token bound to your email address, with the time it was created, the time it expires, and the time it was used

The link expires 15 minutes after it is issued. To limit abuse, we count the sign-in link records already held for your email address in the previous five minutes. That count is calculated from the records listed above and is not stored separately.

Article 3 API Access Data

If you ask for API access, we store a reference for your request, your email address, your organisation name, your intended use, your expected volume, the access tier you are interested in, the status of the request, the date it was made, the date it was reviewed, and any notes we write about the request while reviewing it. Those notes are our own internal comments, and you can ask to see them.

If we invite you, we store the email address the invitation was sent to, the invitation code, the tier it grants, when it was issued, when it expires, whether and when it was used, which API token used it, and any notes we attach to it. If you received an invitation you did not ask for, your email address was given to us by the person or organisation that asked us to invite you, or it was an address you had already given us. You can ask us at any time where your address came from, ask to see any notes, and ask us to delete the record.

If access is granted, we store the API token, its tier, the contact email address and organisation name attached to it, its status, when it was created, when it expires, and any notes we attach to it.

For each API call we keep a usage record: the token, the date and time of the call, the language, the register, the requested length, a non-reversible hash of the request context, the request and item counts, the response status code, any error code, and the response time. Usage records do not contain the text you send or the text we return.

Article 4 Your Inputs and How They Are Processed

Llull Lab does not store the prompts you submit or the content Alberei generates. We process your input only to produce a response, and we do not keep it afterwards.

To produce that response, your input is sent to Anthropic PBC, which operates the language model Alberei calls. This processing takes place on servers outside the European Union. The transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914), under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR. A copy of the safeguards can be requested at the address in Article 1.

Anthropic processes your input under its own terms, which allow it to retain that input for a period it defines, for purposes such as safety and abuse monitoring. That retention is outside our control. When you ask us to delete your data we delete what we hold; we cannot shorten a retention period applied by that provider.

Your input is not used by us for advertising, profiling, behavioural tracking, or to train models. Llull Lab does not systematically monitor inputs or generated outputs, but reserves the right to apply technical or access restrictions where required by law or in cases of misuse.

Article 5 Legal Bases
  • Signing you in, keeping your account, generating a response, and providing API access once granted: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of the service you asked for.
  • Reviewing an API access request or an invitation before any access exists, including the notes we write while reviewing: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in assessing who we grant access to. You may object at any time.
  • Rate limiting, usage records, and serving these pages (which involves your IP address reaching our hosting provider): Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in keeping the service available, secure, and free of abuse. You may object at any time.
  • Transfers outside the European Union: Article 46(2)(c) GDPR, Standard Contractual Clauses.
Article 6 Retention
  • Sign-in links: deleted automatically. A scheduled job runs every hour and removes every sign-in record older than one hour, so such a record exists for at most two hours.
  • API usage records: deleted automatically by the same scheduled job once they are more than 12 months old.
  • Account records, API tokens, invitations, and access requests: these are not deleted automatically. We keep them until you ask us to delete them, and this is true whether or not they are still live: a declined access request, an expired invitation, and a revoked token are all kept on the same terms as active ones.

Deletion requests go to contact@llulllab.com. We act within 30 days and confirm by email. As stated in Article 4, a deletion request cannot reach data held by our model provider under its own retention period.

Article 7 Who Else Processes Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Besides Anthropic PBC (Article 4), the following providers process data on our instructions:

  • GitHub Pages (United States), which serves these pages and, in doing so, receives your IP address.
  • Resend (United States), which delivers the sign-in emails described in Article 2 and therefore processes your email address.
  • Our application database and automation servers, which are located in the European Union.

For the providers located outside the European Union, we rely on the data processing terms each of them publishes, which incorporate the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for the current details at the address in Article 1.

Article 8 Cookies and Local Storage

Alberei sets no cookies. It uses no third-party analytics and no tracking.

When you sign in, a session token is stored in your browser's local storage so that you stay signed in. That token is a signed JSON Web Token and it contains your email address, your internal user identifier, and its issue and expiry times. It is valid for 30 days. This storage is necessary for the service you requested, which is why it does not require consent. You can clear it at any time from your browser, which signs you out.

Article 9 Your Rights (GDPR / CNIL)

In accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and CNIL regulations, you have the following rights:

  • Access to your personal data, including any notes we hold about you, and to know where we obtained it
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your data
  • Restriction of processing, and objection to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Portability of your data
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL

Requests may be sent to: contact@llulllab.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Article 10 Security

Sign-in links are single use and expire after 15 minutes. Account and usage data are held in the European Union, and access to them is restricted. No system can guarantee absolute security.
